## Problem

Traders wanted to act on TradingView signals and their own strategies without watching charts all day, and without risking real money on a setup they had never tested.

## Approach

A platform built around three kinds of bots: Signal Bot for TradingView alerts, FilterEdge Bot for timing entries, and Wallet Bot for sideways markets. Every strategy can be backtested on historical data and run on a demo account before it goes live.

## Build

A multilingual Next.js site with light and dark themes. It connects to Binance, Bybit, OKX, Kraken and XT, and includes take-profit and stop-loss automation and automatic trading reports.

## Result

EnterEdge is live, with templates and tutorials for beginners and deeper settings for experienced traders.

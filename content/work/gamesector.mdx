## Problem

A boosting service needed a steady supply of skilled players, and a clear way for them to apply, get verified and start taking orders.

## Approach

A "Work with us" page that walks applicants through five steps: apply, pass KYC, deliver orders, get paid securely and build a reputation. Open positions can be filtered by game, and "hot offers" highlight the work in highest demand.

## Build

A dark, game-themed Next.js site with optimized images, card layouts for each game and an FAQ covering requirements, timelines and payments.

## Result

The platform is live, with a community of more than 500 boosters across World of Warcraft, Counter-Strike 2, Dota 2, League of Legends, Valorant and other games.

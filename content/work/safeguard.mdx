## Problem

Teams that manage workplace safety track incidents, audits and training in separate spreadsheets and tools, which makes risk hard to see until something goes wrong.

## Approach

Two parts: a marketing site that explains the product, and a dashboard where the work happens. The main Safety Overview puts the key numbers first (incident rate, compliance score, active audits and training hours) and follows them with trends, real-time alerts, recent activity, quarterly goals and the team on duty.

## Build

A Next.js and Tailwind CSS application with light and dark themes. Beyond the safety overview, the dashboard includes further dashboards (Analytics, CRM, Ecommerce, Crypto, Projects and more), apps, several layouts, sign-in and sign-up pages, and a library of UI components, forms and charts. The landing site has features, FAQ, about, contact, privacy and terms pages.

A live demo of the whole product, landing site and dashboard, is available at dashboard.demo.kiabayat.com.

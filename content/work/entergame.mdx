## Problem

Gamers in Iran deal with high ping to European servers, unstable connections during matches, and launchers such as Steam, Epic Games and Battle.net that block logins with errors like HTTP 403. Entergame's Windows client solves these, and it needed a website that explains what the client does and turns visitors into users.

## Approach

A right-to-left Persian site organised around the problems players already know: ping, stability and blocked launchers. Each feature has its own section: Ping Reduction over dedicated routes from Tehran through Istanbul to Frankfurt, Dual Boost over two connections at once, Download Boost for launcher updates, Split Route for sending each app over a different path, and Smart DNS for launcher access.

## Build

A React single-page site built with Vite and Tailwind CSS, in a dark, game-styled design. It includes a library of supported games such as Counter-Strike 2, Valorant, Dota 2 and Fortnite, a pricing section with subscription and Premium plans, a seven-day free trial, an FAQ, a setup guide and pages comparing Entergame with other ping reducers.

## Result

The site is live at entergame.app, with the Windows client available to download from it.

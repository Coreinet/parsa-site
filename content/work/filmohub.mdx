## Problem

Persian-speaking viewers needed one place to find, watch and download movies and TV series in good quality.

## Approach

A right-to-left catalog of movies and series built for fast browsing, with a page for each title that has streaming and download options.

## Build

A Next.js site for a Persian audience, with server rendering so title pages can be found in search.

## Result

FilmoHub is live at filmohub.com.
